## TC-002 — Remove Product from Shopping Cart
# Application: 
SauceDemo 
# Jira ID:
KAN-4 
# Test Objective
Verify that a user can successfully remove a product from the shopping cart.
## Preconditions
- The SauceDemo website is accessible.
- The user is logged in with valid credentials.
- A product has been added to the shopping cart.
- The shopping cart page is accessible.
## Test Steps
-  Open the SauceDemo website.
-  Log in using valid credentials.
-  Select a product and click Add to cart.
-  Open the shopping cart.
-  Click Remove next to the selected product.
-  Verify that the product disappears from the cart.
-  Verify that the shopping cart is empty.
## Expected Result
The selected product is removed and the shopping cart is empty.
## Actual Result
After clicking Remove, the selected product disappeared and the shopping cart was empty.
## Test Status :
PASS
